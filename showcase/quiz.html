Can you spot a phishing email?

Thank you for helping our capstone research. This short quiz shows you ten emails, five phishing and five legitimate. For each one, decide whether it is phishing or legitimate, then read what the model found in the message.

Why we are doing this

Our project simulates how employees in different departments respond to phishing. The simulation needs realistic inputs, and it is only as good as the evidence behind it. Your answers, and how long you take to answer, let us check how the simulated employees' assumptions compare with how people actually judge these emails.

What we record

For each question we record: your answer, whether it was right, and how many milliseconds you took. If you choose, you also tell us your department. We assign a random anonymous ID that is stored in this browser. We do not record your name, your email address, or anything you type in other fields.

Things to know

Every link in these emails is inert. The domains are the reserved example.com domain, which is never registered to anyone and cannot be reached, and the links are not clickable here. Please do not type real passwords or personal details anywhere in this quiz. Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time by closing the tab. Responses already submitted can be withdrawn by sending the anonymous ID shown on the final screen to the team.
